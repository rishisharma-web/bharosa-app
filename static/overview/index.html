Bharosa on Paytm

Udhaar, from a cost into a growth engine.

Every kirana gives credit on trust. Bharosa makes it safe, verified and self-collecting, on the Soundbox the merchant already has and the Paytm app the customer already uses.

Who carries which risk

The shopUdhaar risk stays with the shop, as today, but is capped before goods leave the counter.
The lenderLends against verified receivables only, paid directly to the distributor, repaid automatically from 20% of collections.
The customerShares their score only with consent, sees exactly what a shop sees, and can revoke it.

Protections: a network exposure cap (what a customer owes across all Paytm shops limits new credit), a new-shop discount (40% of the network limit, at most ₹1,500), and a shared-device check on confirmations.

Fraud rings, explained

A dishonest shop can invent “customers”, record round-number udhaar for them, confirm it from one phone and send money back and forth so the fakes look like good payers. The goal: inflate verified receivables to borrow more. Bharosa looks for the tell-tale shape: many customers confirmed from the same device, round amounts, instant confirmations by brand-new customers, and money that leaves the shop and comes straight back. In this prototype a confirmation from a phone already used by another customer at the same shop is flagged and kept out of advance eligibility.

Works on every Paytm Soundbox

Classic SoundboxPrinted QR + speaker + the merchant app. The customer scans the shop QR; the Paytm app finds the waiting request.
AI SoundboxVoice in, a top display for the shopkeeper, and a dynamic QR on the customer-facing screen.

In this prototype the customer web app plays the Paytm app. In production these would be standard Paytm/UPI QR codes read by the Paytm app.

Compliant salary-week auto-pay

UPI AutoPay: the customer approves a mandate with a cap. Each month, the day before collection, Bharosa sends a pre-debit notice with the exact verified dues; the customer can skip that month. On the collection day the merchant side triggers the debit (under ₹15,000 needs no re-authentication), and the lender split applies. The prototype sets up and shows the schedule; it does not move money.
