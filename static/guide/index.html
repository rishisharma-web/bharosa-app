Bharosa on Paytm

How to run the demo

Everything runs at one address. Open the Soundbox on a laptop, the merchant and customer apps on phones, and follow the steps below. The whole demo takes about ten minutes.

A laptopFor the AI Soundbox and the presenter screen
One or two phonesFor the merchant and customer apps. No install: they open in the browser
No phone?The presenter screen has the merchant and customer apps built in

1Open the apps

On a phone, scan the code with the camera app, or type the address. Use the addresses shown here, not localhost.

Merchant app
Lakshmi Stores, the shop. Open on the merchant's phone.
Customer app
The customer's Paytm app, with a QR scanner. Open on the customer's phone.
AI Soundbox
The device on the counter. No login. Open on the laptop.
Presenter screen
The Soundbox, both apps, login QRs, the activity log and Reset. Needs the admin key.

3Before you start

  1. Wake the server. Open the address above and wait for the page to load. A free hosting plan puts the server to sleep after about 15 minutes without visits, and the first visit can take up to a minute.
  2. Reset the demo. On the presenter screen, enter the admin key and press Reset. This restores the starting numbers in the table above in about a second. Without the admin key, skip this step: the server starts from the same numbers whenever it restarts.
  3. Log in. The merchant phone as 9000000001, the customer phone as Ramesh, 9000000012.
  4. Allow the camera on the customer phone the first time you tap Scan QR.
  5. Keep one page open during the demo (the presenter screen or the Soundbox), so the server doesn't go back to sleep.

5Only a laptop?

Open the presenter screen. It shows the Soundbox and the merchant and customer apps side by side. Log in to both phone frames with the numbers above. Instead of scanning, click the large QR on the Soundbox: it opens the same request in a new tab, logged in as the customer in the frame.

6If something goes wrong

You seeWhy, and what to do
"This QR is for payments. Use your UPI app to pay."You scanned the shop's normal "Scan to pay" QR. Tap a chip and Use suggestion first, then scan the large QR that appears.
"This request is for another customer."The phone is logged in as someone else. Tap Log out and log in as the customer named on the Soundbox.
"This request has expired."Requests last 15 minutes. Tap the chip on the Soundbox again.
The camera doesn't openAllow camera access for the site in the browser. Or tap Open on the "Action needed" card instead of scanning.
A ₹340 request comes back red for Ramesh, or the numbers look wrongEarlier runs changed the data. Press Reset on the presenter screen.
Suddenly logged out, and the numbers are back to the startThe server restarted (for example after sleeping). Log in again; the demo starts from the beginning.
The first page load takes a long timeThe server is waking up. Wait up to a minute.
A screen looks stalePull down to refresh, or reload the page.
The presenter screen asks for an admin keyAsk the team. The merchant, customer and Soundbox apps don't need it.

7What's real and what's simulated

Real, in this prototype
The Bharosa Score, safe limits and every rupee figure, computed live from the ledger
Customer confirmation by QR, disputes, consent to share a score
Oldest-debt-first repayments and the 20% lender split
AI (Sarvam) for language only, behind validators, when switched on
Simulated
No real money moves: UPI, the credit line and the lender are stand-ins
The OTP is always 123456; no SMS is sent
Auto-pay is set up and scheduled, but never collects
The date is fixed at Sat 31 Oct 2026, so the overdue days and the Diwali countdown stay the same
Concept prototype · Team MerchantMavericks. All names, shops, the lender and the distributor are fictional.
