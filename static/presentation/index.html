Guardrail (40 s, demo). On the Soundbox tap Priya 340 (green), then Ramesh 340 (amber) and tap Why?: score 662 gives a base of ₹3,215, ×0.6 for 19 days overdue, minus ₹1,700 already owed, so ₹200 on credit and ₹140 now. How it works (45 s). Read top to bottom. AI sits only where input is messy: speech, the shopkeeper's words, customers' replies, and wording the plan. Every AI output goes through a validator: the format must be right, the customer must exist, and every rupee must match a figure the code computed. If anything fails or Sarvam takes over 8 s, a rule or template answers, and the badge on screen shows which. Score, limits, payments, the split and the advance are plain code. Who carries which risk

The shop keeps its udhaar risk as today, but capped and informed. Advances and above-limit credit sit with regulated partners. Paytm takes no balance-sheet risk.

Risks, designed out

The questions we expect, answered by design.

Isn't this a khata app?

Khata apps record. Bharosa checks risk before credit, gets the customer's confirmation, wins new customers, collects and funds stock.

Is AI deciding credit?

No. A transparent formula suggests; the shopkeeper decides. AI only handles language, and every output is checked by code.

Isn't sharing scores regulated?

Likely. So sharing is consent-based and revocable, disclosure is minimal, and production scoring runs through a licensed partner.

Who takes the loss on a default?

The shop, as today, but with far better information and a capped limit. Paytm carries no credit risk.

Can shops fake receivables?

Shared devices, instant confirmations, round amounts and circular money are flagged and kept out of advance eligibility.

Does it need new hardware?

No. Any Paytm Soundbox and the printed QR work; the AI Soundbox adds voice and screens.

Risks (30 s, or skip to Q&A). Answer from this slide when the questions come. The two that usually come first: "Is AI deciding credit?" (no: show the Why? reasons, which are code) and "Can shops fake receivables?" (only customer-confirmed entries count, and the prototype already flags a phone confirming for two customers).
